## Services

- Gas hob stays on the wall; island is kept free of gas.
- Hood/extraction must be ducted or otherwise engineered independently from comfort AC.
- Island sink center remains a compact relocation relative to the earlier kitchen wet zone, but supply, waste fall, cleanout and route remain MEP-TBD.
- Dishwasher is adjacent to the sink by design.
- Island electrical: provide service allowance for dishwasher plus countertop/general-use power only after local electrical design.
- Do not place sockets where they conflict with sink splash zones or drawers.
