# K4 Electrical / Low-Voltage / Appliance Coordination

Status: **coordination intent only**. Final circuits, protective devices, cable sizes, bathroom zones and installation must be designed/approved by the responsible licensed electrical engineer under applicable Egyptian/project requirements.

## Kitchen / open plan

### Dedicated appliance/service points
- fridge/freezer
- dishwasher in island
- built-in washer in west tall-wall module
- under-counter oven
- gas hob ignition / controls as required
- ducted hood
- microwave / appliance-garage provision in pantry module
- under-cabinet lighting driver
- optional water filter / purifier below sink

### Island
- Provide concealed power feed coordinated with sink/DW plumbing route.
- Target at least two convenient user outlets on a non-wet island face; locate away from sink splash zone and visible living elevation.
- USB-C can be integrated with one outlet cluster if desired.
- Do not use a pop-up outlet in the worktop unless a selected product solves waterproofing, cleaning and replacement access convincingly.

### Counter / pantry
- Multiple small-appliance outlets at usable worktop height, not hidden behind appliances.
- Pantry/appliance-garage outlet(s) for microwave / coffee machine / blender charging.
- Leave service access to all appliance isolation points; do not bury them behind non-removable joinery.

## Living / dining / entry
- TV: power + Ethernet/data + conduit reserve; hide future device cabling.
- Router / access-point strategy should be planned centrally rather than relying on the TV cabinet Wi-Fi location.
- Side tables / floor-lamp zone: convenient switched/unswitched outlets.
- Dining sideboard: outlet for lamp / serving appliances.
- Entry cabinet: outlet for charging / cordless vacuum if practical.
- Prewire living curtain/blind motor only if motorisation remains a real option.

## Bedrooms
- Both sides of main bed: power + USB-C / charging; independent reading-light switching.
- Desks: multiple power + Ethernet/data provision.
- TV point only where genuinely wanted; do not force a TV wall in every bedroom.
- Wardrobes/dressing: low-voltage LED driver locations accessible for service, ideally sensor controlled.
- Optional curtain motor conduit/power before plastering.

## Bathrooms / WC
- Mirror lighting and demister provision only if selected.
- Shaver/toothbrush outlet strategy subject to local bathroom electrical-zone requirements.
- Exhaust fan point and control if mechanical ventilation is required.
- Water-heater point only if the actual hot-water strategy requires local heaters; do not assume.
- No ad-hoc outlet placement in wet zones.

## HVAC
- Dedicated feeds for selected indoor/outdoor units.
- Condensate pumps, controls, thermostats and service access coordinated with G/K service pocket design.
- Do not finalise ceiling openings or grille sizes before heat-load and equipment selection.

## Service terraces
- Weather-rated service outlet(s) only if permitted/needed.
- Main terrace: outdoor-rated outlet and optional lighting, positioned clear of drainage/water.
- Service terrace remains drying/utility in K4; no washer power point is required there if K3 indoor laundry is adopted, but a future utility outlet may still be useful.

## Switching / controls
- Prefer neutral conductors at key switch boxes if smart/dimming controls may be added later.
- Living/dining: at least separate control groups for general track, accent/lamp circuit where hardwired, and kitchen.
- Two-way/scene switching at sensible circulation points rather than forcing phone/app control.
- Night path: low-level hall/bath guidance may use motion sensors, but avoid nuisance activation into bedrooms.

## Pre-plaster hold point
Before closing walls/ceilings, issue a coordinated drawing overlaying furniture, joinery, plumbing, HVAC, lighting, switches, sockets, data, curtain tracks and access panels. Photograph and dimension concealed routes before closure.
