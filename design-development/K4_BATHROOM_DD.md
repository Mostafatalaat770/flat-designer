# K4 Bathroom Design Development

Status: **layout direction retained; waterproofing / plumbing / door systems not construction approved**

## Master ensuite — preferred daily-use bathroom

Room source: ~1.70 × 2.75 m.

### Layout
- Walk-in shower envelope: **1500 × 800 mm** at the north end.
- WC: retained at south/east zone.
- Vanity: 600 mm retained at south/west zone.
- Dressing → ensuite opening study: target wall gap **~900 mm** so the final pocket/sliding system has a realistic chance of achieving a comfortable clear opening after frame/cassette losses.

### Development direction
- Fixed glass panel + open entry preferred over a hinged shower door if splash testing allows.
- Target a generous open entry; do not create a narrow decorative glass maze.
- Linear or well-positioned point drain selected only after slab/fall/plumbing review.
- Full waterproofing to the shower zone and appropriate upstands/returns per responsible wet-area specification.
- One recessed niche where wall/services permit; do not cut structural concrete.
- Hand shower + overhead shower; mixer/valve location reachable without standing directly under initial cold water.
- Towel hook/rail near shower exit, not behind the pocket-door travel.
- Vanity mirror with face-friendly vertical light.
- Storage: vanity drawers + mirrored/medicine storage if needed rather than widening the vanity until the WC feels pinched.

### Measured spatial note
The current model leaves roughly **1.06 m** from the shower envelope to the beginning of the WC envelope, giving a useful dry standing zone.

## Family bathroom — family capability retained

### Layout
- Bathtub: **1500 × 750 mm**.
- WC: retained on east side.
- Vanity: 600 mm retained.
- Hall → bath opening study: ~800 mm wall gap with sliding/pocket strategy remains **CONDITIONAL**; actual clear opening will be smaller after the frame/system.

### Development direction
- Tub supports children/family/soaking and prevents the apartment from losing bathtub capability.
- Provide a fixed or folding glass screen for shower-over-bath use if desired.
- Hand shower + bath filler / thermostatic mixer per selected system.
- Add a bath-side niche/shelf only where the wall build-up allows it.
- Reinstate practical towel/cleaning storage as wall-mounted or vanity-integrated storage rather than a floor cabinet that obstructs circulation.
- Brushed nickel/chrome preferred for maintainability and replacement availability.
- Mirror lighting independent from general ceiling light.

### Spatial note
The model retains roughly **570 mm** between the family-tub north edge and the WC envelope. Final standing/transfer comfort must be checked against the actual selected WC projection and tub/screen.

## Guest WC

### Layout
- Existing modeled opening ~657 mm retained in this pass.
- Door studied **opening outward** to remove the interior door/basin conflict.
- Compact wall-hung handrinse: **350 × 180 mm** envelope.
- WC retained.

### Development direction
- Keep the basin genuinely compact and wall-hung.
- Shallow mirrored cabinet is preferable to deep storage.
- Door handle/stop must not hit entry/dining furniture when open.
- Provide mechanical extract if natural ventilation is not adequate / required by the final MEP design.
- Warmest decorative treatment can live here: restrained bronze/brass, warmer mirror light, one durable feature surface.

## Wet-area coordination gate

Before tile order or rough-in:
1. exact door/pocket systems;
2. actual fixture SKUs and projections;
3. drain positions and invert levels;
4. hot/cold feed and isolation locations;
5. waterproofing build-up and thresholds;
6. floor falls;
7. glass dimensions/hinges;
8. ventilation/extract;
9. lighting/electrical wet-zone requirements;
10. towel/accessory fixing backing.

No generic render is evidence for waterproofing, drain fall, structural cutting or code compliance.
