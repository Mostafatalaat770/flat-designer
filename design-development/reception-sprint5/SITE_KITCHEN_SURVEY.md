- [ ] Exact **Egypt-market** product/model and latest manufacturer installation guide attached for *each* selected device
- [ ] DW is **truly fully integrated** for matching cabinet front, or cabinet explicitly redesigned for freestanding appliance
- [ ] Manufacturer's full **door hinge / basket opening drawing** obtained; overall open depth alone is insufficient
- [ ] As-built open widths and finished floor levels checked independently of 600mm furniture proxy
- [ ] Oven under gas hob permitted by the **chosen hob+oven manufacturer** (heat shield, aperture, cooling, gas and electrics)
- [ ] Kitchen and terrace door continuous sweeps checked with two occupants, tray, product doors and projected hardware
- [ ] Qualified local MEP verified extraction outlet, make-up air, drains, water shutoffs, moisture control, rated protected circuits and safe service access
- [ ] Developer and qualified structural professional signed any actual P1/wall44 proposal in writing
- [ ] Owner/family reviewed 1:1 physical mockup, practical storage, cleaning and maintenance
- [ ] Final coordinated CAD elevations, shop drawings, product data sheets, MEP plans, BOQ and owner approval issued **before** purchase/install

**Return:** filled form, calibrated photos/measurements and maker cutout templates. Without them, keep P0 and status **EXPLORATORY / HOLD**, not "construction ready." Never ask occupants to touch live circuits or gas to complete this survey.
