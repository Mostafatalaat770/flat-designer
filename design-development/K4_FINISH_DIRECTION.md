# K4 Finish Direction

Status: **DESIGN DIRECTION / SAMPLES REQUIRED**

## Public open plan
- Floor: continuous warm limestone/travertine-look porcelain direction across living, dining and kitchen.
- Reason: supports the full-open architecture and avoids a leftover kitchen-only tile boundary.
- Final tile must be judged from full-size physical samples; current render texture is not a product selection.
- Walls: warm off-white / soft greige.
- Island: natural oak furniture-grade face.
- Main kitchen cabinetry: soft warm ivory.
- Worktop/backsplash: quiet warm quartz/porcelain/stone-look, low movement.

## Private zone
- Bedrooms + private hall: parquet/wood direction retained for warmth and acoustics.
- Dressing: same private-zone wood continuity unless threshold/wet-risk detailing requires otherwise.

## Bathrooms
- Durable warm porcelain family.
- Anti-slip wet-area flooring.
- Minimal grout contrast; grout and silicone colors coordinated to tile.

## Metals
- Keep one dominant metal family per room.
- Avoid mixing black, chrome, brass and bronze everywhere.
- Kitchen appliance black/glass is treated as equipment, not a reason to make all hardware black.

## Soft finishes
- Curtains: full-height wherever glazing permits.
- Living: layered sheer + blackout/privacy strategy as needed.
- Bedrooms: blackout capability prioritized.
- Rugs remain removable warmth/acoustic layers rather than compensating for a cold base palette.

## Sample approval sequence
1. Floor tile full-size sample.
2. Oak veneer/laminate sample.
3. Ivory cabinet sample.
4. Worktop slab sample.
5. Wall paint large brush-out.
6. Bathroom tile + grout + metal combination.
7. Review all samples together under 3000 K evening light and daylight before ordering.
