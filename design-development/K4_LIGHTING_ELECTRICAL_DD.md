# K4 Lighting + Electrical Design Development

Status: **DESIGN DEVELOPMENT / SITE + ELECTRICAL ENGINEER REVIEW REQUIRED**

## Global principles
- 3000 K default throughout living, bedrooms and bathrooms unless task-specific.
- Prefer high-CRI fixtures (target CRI 90+).
- Avoid a uniform ceiling grid of downlights in the reception.
- Separate ambient / task / accent circuits so the apartment can be dim at night without losing function.
- Final outlet counts, RCD/RCBO selection, earthing, wet-zone rules and circuit loading follow the electrical engineer and local requirements.

## Reception / dining
Lighting layers:
1. Track / directional ambient lighting for living and art.
2. Floor/table lamps at sofa and lounge chair.
3. Dining table receives aimed track lighting; decorative pendant remains optional, not required.
4. TV wall gets low-glare ambient/accent only; avoid glare directly opposite screen.

Power/data allowances:
- TV wall: power + data + spare conduit; allowance for router/streaming/console/soundbar.
- Sofa zone: accessible socket/USB-C near both sofa ends without floor trailing cables.
- Lounge chair/side table: one accessible power point for lamp/charging.
- Dining: no floor socket unless a real use is established.
- Entry shoe cabinet: optional low-voltage/power for charging/vacuum only if joinery requires it.

## Kitchen
Dedicated/controlled points:
- Refrigerator.
- Oven.
- Dishwasher.
- Hood/extractor.
- Gas ignition / hob accessories as required.
- Countertop small-appliance outlets distributed across both prep sides, not directly behind the hob or sink.
- Island power only on the kitchen-facing/side panel after sink splash zones and drawer construction are fixed.
- Spare circuit/conduit for water filtration / disposer only if actually selected.

Lighting:
- Aimed ceiling track/task lighting.
- Under-cabinet task strip to the west cooking/prep wall.
- No decorative pendant over island in the current low-clutter direction.
- Hood task light counts as cooking task layer.

## Master bedroom
- Two-way switching at entry and both bedside positions.
- Each bedside: power + USB-C / charging provision.
- Reading light independent on each side.
- Curtain/blind motor provision only if motorized curtains are selected.
- TV/data provision only if the owner confirms a bedroom TV.

## Bedroom 1
- Desk: minimum two general sockets + data/conduit + task light.
- Bedside charging provision.
- Wardrobe internal light on door sensor optional.
- Main ambient + task desk circuit separated if practical.

## Bedroom 2
- Desk: minimum two general sockets + data/conduit + task light.
- Bedside charging provision.
- Wardrobe internal light optional.
- Preserve flexibility for future child/guest use; do not hard-wire around one desk layout more than necessary.

## Dressing
- Even vertical illumination at wardrobe fronts.
- Avoid only overhead downlights, which cast facial/body shadows.
- One accessible service socket for steamer/iron/hair appliance only if safe and useful.

## Bathrooms / guest WC
- Mirror/face lighting on a separate task circuit from general ceiling light.
- Exhaust fan control coordinated with light/timer/humidity strategy.
- Shaver/toothbrush outlet only where wet-zone rules permit.
- Master and family **shower** wet zones: no outlet positioning until waterproofing and wet-zone drawings are frozen. The earlier family bathtub is an archived K4 alternative, not the current 900×900mm shower source.
- Guest WC: mirror task lighting prioritized because the handrinse is compact.

## Hall
- Low-glare track or compact ceiling lighting.
- Night path lighting optional; avoid over-lighting the corridor.

## Service terrace / laundry
- Washer on dedicated protected circuit with suitable isolation and RCD/RCBO per engineer/local requirement.
- Outdoor/weather exposure rating required for any socket or isolator.
- No assumption that a normal indoor socket is acceptable on the terrace.

## Controls
- Reception: at least separate Living Ambient / Accent-Lamps / Dining / Kitchen Task controls.
- Dimming preferred on living/dining ambient circuits.
- Bedrooms: entry + bedside two-way controls.
- Bathrooms: general and mirror/task separate; exhaust strategy coordinated.

## Before electrical first-fix
1. Freeze joinery elevations and exact appliance SKUs.
2. Freeze TV position and media equipment.
3. Freeze curtain type/motorization.
4. Freeze bathroom mirror/vanity sizes.
5. Issue coordinated reflected-ceiling + power + low-voltage drawings.
