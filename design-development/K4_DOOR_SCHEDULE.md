# K4 Door / Opening Design Schedule

Status: **DESIGN DEVELOPMENT / EXACT SYSTEMS TBD**

All dimensions below are modeled architectural study dimensions. Final clear openings must be measured after frame, pocket cassette, plaster/tile and tolerance build-ups.

## Guest WC — OP-RECP-WC

Current modeled opening:
- Approx. 657 mm canonical opening.
- Preferred strategy: **outward swing into reception side**, not inward into the compact WC.

Why:
- Protects the compact 350 × 180 mm handrinse.
- Recovers usable floor/standing space inside WC.
- Avoids door/vanity conflict identified in earlier studies.

Conditions:
- Check that the outward leaf does not create a dangerous conflict with circulation, furniture or the entry route.
- Handle/stop position must prevent wall/furniture damage.
- Do not widen unless site/structure gives a clear reason; this is currently the low-demolition option.

## Family bathroom — OP-HALL-BATHC

Canonical opening:
- Approx. 657 mm.

Architectural study:
- Widened render-only wall gap around **800 mm**.
- Hinged leaf suppressed to study sliding/pocket behavior.

Preferred direction:
- **Sliding / pocket-like solution** if the selected system can achieve a useful clear opening without compromising waterproofing/services.

Important:
- 800 mm wall gap is **not** 800 mm clear passage after cassette/frame.
- Pocket cassette requires sufficient uninterrupted wall length.
- Switches, plumbing and waterproofing cannot occupy the cassette zone without coordination.
- If a proper pocket system is too tight, use a quality surface slider rather than forcing a bad pocket detail.

## Master dressing → ensuite — OP-WIC-BATHM

Architectural study:
- Render-only wall gap increased to **900 mm**.
- Preferred strategy: pocket/sliding door.

Why:
- Better daily access from dressing to ensuite.
- Avoids a hinged leaf consuming a narrow dressing/bath interface.
- 900 mm wall gap gives more realistic allowance for frame/system losses than the earlier 800 mm study.

Conditions:
- Exact clear opening depends on selected system.
- Wet-area wall build-up and pocket cassette must be detailed together.
- Avoid burying plumbing/electrical items in the pocket cavity.
- Provide serviceable hardware from a locally supported system.

## Bedroom doors

Direction:
- Retain canonical hinged-door arrangement unless site measurement reveals a conflict.
- No architectural benefit established from converting ordinary bedroom doors to sliders.

## Main entry

Direction:
- Retain canonical entry door/opening.
- H2 dining shift protects the entry route without changing the main door.
- Final lock, security, threshold and fire/authority requirements follow developer/building rules.

## General hardware principles

- Consistent lever/handle family apartment-wide.
- Bathroom privacy locks with emergency release.
- Soft-close / soft-open where the selected sliding system supports it.
- Avoid floor tracks in bathrooms where possible; prefer top-hung systems with appropriate guides.
- Door stops positioned to protect walls and joinery.
- Final door heights should coordinate with the 2.50 m clear ceiling and wall proportions rather than being chosen only from renderer defaults.

## Before ordering doors

1. Verify structural/non-structural status of any widened opening.
2. Site-measure rough opening after demolition/finishes are known.
3. Select exact pocket/surface-slide system.
4. Draw cassette, frame, tile/plaster and skirting build-up.
5. Coordinate switches, plumbing, waterproofing and wall-mounted accessories.
6. Confirm final clear opening, not just rough opening.
7. Check every open leaf/slider against furniture and circulation.
