- Soft warm ivory main fronts.
- Natural-oak reception-facing island back storage.

- Integrated appliance fronts wherever practical.
- Drawers preferred over deep shelves for lower storage.
- Blum AMBIA-LINE remains a reference for internal organization, not a purchased specification.

## Decision rule

Prefer K3 over service-terrace laundry if the indoor laundry module can be serviced cleanly without unacceptable noise/storage loss. Use the service terrace for drying/utility unless a specific washer is documented for the actual outdoor exposure and enclosure conditions.
