# K4 Appliance Shortlist — Egypt market check

Checked: **7 October 2026**  
Status: **CURRENT REFERENCE SHORTLIST / NOT YET PROCUREMENT-LOCKED**

This shortlist is used to prove the K4 joinery envelopes against products currently listed by Bosch Egypt. Availability and pricing can change before apartment fit-out.

## 1. Gas hob — preferred current reference

**Bosch Series 6 PCP6A6I90**
- 60 cm gas hob
- black enameled steel
- 4 usable gas positions
- FlameSelect
- continuous cast-iron supports
- flame-failure safety cut-off
- Bosch Egypt: available for order at the time checked
- Listed price at check: EGP 18,999 incl. VAT

Published installation drawing:
- overall: approx. **582 × 520 mm**
- cut-out width: **560 +2/-0 mm**
- cut-out depth: **480–490 +2/-0 mm**
- manufacturer drawing shows minimum distance to wall / cabinet constraints that must be followed.

Why it fits K4:
- sits naturally in the developed 600 mm hob base
- black finish works with the black hood/oven accents
- gas remains on the wall, not the island

Official source:
https://www.bosch-home.com/eg/en/product/PCP6A6I90

## 2. Hood — preferred current reference

**Bosch Series 6 DWK67PJ60T**
- 60 cm black wall-mounted glass hood
- max extraction: **510 m³/h**
- boost extraction: **700 m³/h**
- sound power at max speed: **59 dB**
- Bosch Egypt: available for order at the time checked
- Listed price at check: EGP 16,999 incl. VAT

Installation manual guidance:
- avoid long/rough exhaust runs and excessive bends
- round **150 mm** exhaust pipe is a preferred reference size; equivalent flat duct cross-section approx. 177 cm²
- exhaust pipe should be installed to avoid condensate return per manufacturer instructions

Why it fits K4:
- matches the 600 mm hood zone
- strong enough to justify engineering around a ducted solution rather than default recirculation
- K4 has a particularly short ~1.19 m plan route from hob line to the service-terrace wall, which is favorable for pressure/noise performance

Official source:
https://www.bosch-home.com/eg/en/product/cookingandbaking/cookerhoods/wallmountedcookerhoods/DWK67PJ60T

## Procurement rule

Do **not** manufacture final appliance openings from this note alone.

Before shop drawing approval:
1. Recheck current Egyptian availability.
2. Download exact installation manuals for the final SKUs.
3. Give the joiner the exact appliance drawings.
4. Confirm ventilation, hinge, filler and door-swing requirements.
5. Confirm electrical/gas service requirements.
6. Recheck prices only when the purchase window is near.
