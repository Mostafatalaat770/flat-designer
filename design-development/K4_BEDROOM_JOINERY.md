## Joinery construction principles

- Site-measure after plaster/tiles are known.
- Use fillers/scribes at walls rather than forcing nominal widths.
- Coordinate skirting and door architraves before carcass production.
- Ventilate enclosed appliance/utility joinery where required.
- Use moisture-resistant materials in wet-adjacent locations.
- Provide removable/service panels where services are concealed.
- Prefer full-extension drawers and good-quality hinges/runners.
- Avoid overly deep shelves above eye level where items disappear.
