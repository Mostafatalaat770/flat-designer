# K4 Materials + Lighting Direction

Status: **Design development direction — samples and technical approval required**

## Public open plan

### Floor
- One continuous visual floor across living / dining / kitchen.
- Preferred direction: premium **warm light-oak-look porcelain / ceramic plank** or equivalent kitchen-suitable hard surface.
- The current `lightoak` renderer material is only a visual proxy.
- Final sample must be judged full-size under 3000 K lighting and daylight.
- Priorities: rectified edges, convincing grain variation, low visual repetition, stain resistance, easy cleaning, kitchen suitability, verified slip performance and locally supportable replacement stock.

### Walls
- Use one coherent warm-neutral family across reception and kitchen.
- Living finish: low-sheen / matte washable paint.
- Kitchen exposed walls: same colour family in a more washable finish where required.
- Avoid strong feature-wall colour changes that recreate the demolished kitchen boundary.

### Kitchen
- Main fronts: soft warm ivory.
- Island reception face / shallow storage: natural oak.
- Worktop + backsplash: warm off-white quartz / sintered-stone direction; Dekton Aeris remains a reference, not a selection.
- Hardware: minimal integrated pulls / shadow gaps; avoid decorative handles competing with the open-plan living room.
- Metal accents: restrained dark bronze / black only where functional; do not turn every fitting black.

### Living / dining joinery
- Keep the darker dining sideboard as a contrast piece only if its final elevation remains visually light.
- Natural oak should remain the dominant wood family.
- Upholstery: warm off-white / mushroom with muted olive accent.
- Curtains: full-height layered sheer + drapery at living glazing, mounted as high and wide as the verified opening allows.

## Private zone

### Bedrooms / hall / dressing
- Retain natural-oak/parquet direction for a warmer private zone.
- Warm ivory walls.
- Linen / mushroom textiles; restrained olive or earthy accent.
- Avoid repeating the exact same artwork/colour accent in every room.

### Bathrooms
- Large-format warm stone-look porcelain on primary wall/floor fields.
- Anti-slip finish in shower/tub wet zones.
- Master: pale oak or warm ivory vanity + restrained bronze/nickel.
- Family bath: lighter neutral + brushed nickel/chrome for easier replacement/maintenance.
- Guest WC may carry the slightly warmer/brass accent because it is small and dry.

## Lighting principles

- Default CCT: **3000 K** for the apartment unless a selected task fixture requires a deliberate exception.
- Prefer CRI 90+ for primary interior lighting.
- Use dimming in living/dining/master where practical.
- Layer light: ambient + task + accent. Avoid a uniform ceiling grid.

### Living / dining
- Slim track or magnetic-track direction, aimed at walls/art/coffee zone rather than straight down everywhere.
- Floor lamp + sideboard/table lamp remain part of the lighting layer.
- No mandatory chandelier. Keep dining ceiling visually calm; a future slim linear pendant is optional, not required.
- Provide a dim scene for evenings and a brighter cleaning/general scene.

### Kitchen
- Track/task lighting over work areas.
- Continuous under-cabinet LED at the west cooking/prep run, concealed from direct view.
- Dedicated task light to sink/island.
- Hood lighting remains task-only.
- Avoid pendant clutter over the island because dining is immediately adjacent.

### Bedrooms
- Bedside reading lights / sconces + a restrained general layer.
- Reduce reliance on many downlights; use softer lamp/sconce light for normal evening use.
- Dressing wardrobe LEDs on door/sensor control.

### Bathrooms
- Ceiling general light + vertical/face-friendly mirror light.
- Shower/tub wet-zone fittings selected to the required local ingress/safety rating.
- Avoid relying on a ceiling downlight directly over the mirror as the only face light.

## Sample gate

Do not release flooring, paint, stone, joinery laminate/veneer or metal finish from screen colours. Approve physical samples together under daylight and 3000 K artificial light, and retain a signed sample board for site matching.
