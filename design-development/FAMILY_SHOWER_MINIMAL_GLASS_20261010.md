# Family bath · less glass hardware, more daily comfort

**Type B 10 Oct 2026**. Candidate **F1 minimal glass** is a **render-only hardware study** on the **exact 900×900mm family shower**. The saved cloud v55 and canonical partitions, plumbing, 2.50m ceiling, 656.7mm nominal bathroom doorway, and all WC/vanity positions are unchanged.

## Why change this?

The present `style` shower draws an opaque high tray and a cage of dark rails, with generic glass on sides which can face original masonry. Actual family shower corner is at the **north/east end** of BATH-C01 (room boundaries x=7181..8982mm, y=6207..8960mm). The faces that should be reviewed for the enclosure are **west (toward room circulation) and south (toward WC)**. No extra west-side ground cabinet should reduce circulation.

## F0 vs F1

- **F0 existing:** 900mm square, existing high dark-framed render, current unchanged bathroom and shelves. Historical baseline.
- **F1 conditional:** same shower, **900mm x 900mm**, trial 22mm low-profile pale base, 1980mm clear glazing, slim brushed nickel clips **no heavy top bar**, south pane (full) and west pane (330mm fixed rear plus 570mm visually CLOSED leaf). All numbers are *parametric preview only*, not dimensions to order; physical tray buildup may force a taller threshold. Actual hinge/pivot/folding, supporting wall and water seals are **TBD**.

**High-risk collision**: shower west edge x≈7959.6mm and west room wall x≈7181mm leave **~779mm nominal**. A naive 570mm west-side *outward* glass swing would leave only **~209mm** to the wall at maximum projection — inappropriate occupied passage and no door-angle/standing-room safety approval. Thus **NEVER infer outward-opening hardware from a beautiful CLOSED image**. Ask the glass maker for safe inward/bifold/pivot options and the required passage and anti-scald/wet splash/cleaning design, or reject the alternative.

The south edge to current WC footprint is ~320mm nominal; no claim a person can use this as an aisle. Nominal hall doorway is ~657mm and may be even smaller after finishes, so physically test family access and door swing. Present models are not an exact human circulation simulation.

## Architectural and waterproofing hold

The 22mm base is a **render-only ambition**. Supplier/qualified architect/MEP must prove tray/screed/slab route, accessible drain/trap, compatible wall/floor waterproofing, fully sealed shower door, wet-use tile resistance, field ponding/flood test, and safe glass anchoring. No drilling/cutting slab or moving drains from the render. Preserve true 2.50m clear ceiling.

## Design character

The result should be **quiet warm stone + transparent safety glass + minimal brushed-nickel metal + closed pale vanity storage + one shallow dry cabinet**, not black industrial cage hardware or overcrowded walls. Keep the existing easy-clean metal shower shelf at nominal 1250mm only if the actual wall is suitable for fixings and waterproofing.

## How to judge

The dedicated GitHub workflow draws **FOUR real WebGL frames**: original F0 vs minimal-glass F1, same saved v55 `bath55-family-entry` and `bath55-family-dry-cabinet` cameras. It emits a source-dimensioned explanatory SVG and structured hard approval gates. Do not promote F1 to live studio/current snapshot or choose glass/tray/skirting SKU just because camera shots improve. Master and guest bathrooms retain previous separate review status.
