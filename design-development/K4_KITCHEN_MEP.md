# K4 Kitchen MEP / Extraction Development

Status: **PREFERRED ROUTE / ENGINEERING APPROVAL REQUIRED**

## Extraction route

The west cooking wall is unusually favorable for a short ducted hood route.

Model coordinates:
- Hob centre: approximately **x=9381 mm, y=7774 mm**
- Kitchen/service-terrace boundary: **y=8960 mm**
- The boundary segment directly in line with the hob spans **x=8981–9765 mm**

Therefore the hob centre projects directly onto a full-height kitchen/service-terrace wall segment. The plan-distance from hob centre to that boundary is only about:

**8960 - 7774 = 1186 mm**

This supports a preferred concept:

1. Wall-mounted 600 mm gas hob.
2. Ducted hood above hob.
3. Rise within/above the 600 mm hood zone.
4. Short high-level duct run roughly 1.2 m toward the service terrace.
5. High-level sleeve/grille into the service terrace/exterior discharge strategy.

This is materially better than routing extraction across the reception ceiling.

## Why this matters

- Keeps grease/odor extraction independent from comfort AC.
- Avoids a long duct across the open-plan living space.
- Preserves the 2.50 m reception ceiling.
- Keeps the hob on the existing/service side rather than moving gas onto the island.
- Makes the full-open kitchen more credible for daily Egyptian cooking.

## Hard gates

- Confirm the kitchen/service-terrace wall is permitted to receive the required sleeve/penetration.
- Confirm exact hood duct diameter and manufacturer maximum equivalent length.
- Confirm external termination is allowed and does not discharge toward glazing/door openings in a way that causes recirculation.
- Confirm fire/gas/developer requirements.
- Avoid excessive bends; final route should be drawn in section.
- Provide make-up air strategy appropriate to hood flow and apartment airtightness.
- Do not connect hood exhaust to comfort-AC return/supply or shared shafts unless specifically designed/approved.

## Hood design target

The current joinery reserves a **600 mm hood zone** centered over a 600 mm hob. Final selection should prioritize:
- genuine ducted operation,
- strong capture at practical noise levels,
- washable metal grease filters,
- easy service access,
- locally supported spare filters/motors,
- duct outlet compatible with the short service-terrace route.

If a selected hood requires a wider canopy, the 1800 mm upper composition can be revised before fabrication; the base-cabinet 600/600/600 grid does not need to change.

## Plumbing

The island sink remains close to the original wet-service zone. Final plumbing must document:
- hot/cold supply route,
- waste diameter and fall,
- cleanout/service access,
- dishwasher connection adjacent to sink,
- no inaccessible joints buried under finished floor unless the plumbing design explicitly permits them.

## Gas

Gas stays on the west wall. Final design requires:
- exact approved gas point,
- isolation valve access,
- hob clearances per selected manual,
- separation from electrical outlets,
- ventilation / safety requirements per local gas authority and responsible engineer.
