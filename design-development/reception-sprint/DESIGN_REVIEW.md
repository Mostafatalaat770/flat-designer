## Observations from the actual images

- **Entry / living→dining:** the shifted buffet, single landscape artwork and retained oval table form a clearer composition. A's empty volume above the table feels calmer at 2500mm. B's bar is an intentional focal element, but the entry view shows how it crosses the terrace sightline. The camera retains the real partition edge rather than removing geometry for presentation.
- **Dining→living / wide:** light public flooring connects the lounge and full-height glazing; the retained 2880mm sofa and olive chair give A a restrained colour anchor. The real partition and narrow hall remain visible. No extra reception area has been invented. The broad dining wall remains a sample/detail opportunity, not justification for new circulation-blocking joinery.
- **Kitchen relationship P0/P1:** the P0 leaf and return obscure the sink run; P1 visibly reveals that run and improves the arrival-to-kitchen sightline. It also exposes the service-door brown tone and real working kitchen to reception. The work-run frame stays identical between topologies because appliances/cabinets are unchanged. Better openness does not solve odor closure or the operating conflicts.
- **Evening:** A has a legible sofa/reading corner and a strong lamp pool on the left wall; B is more subdued and needs a better real reading-light balance. Both need dimming and beam trials. The terrace glass appears dark/reflective; the images are not a solar or glare calculation.
- **Seated TV:** the screen is readable at the exact 1100mm eye proxy, with the original AC above and curtain at the left. The corrected console tint and coordinated buffet make the A/B material distinction visible. Source slats and tabletop accessories remain: confirm dust access, cable ventilation and reflections, rather than treating a styled proxy as finished joinery.

The standing images show the saved chair positions, not six rendered human bodies or opened appliance meshes. The separate dimensioned +200/+350/+450 plans and audit explicitly test those operating envelopes. Use both forms of evidence; the attractive closed-state images cannot certify the busy family state.

**Reject:** historical K12/K4 coordinate imports, a new fixed divider in the serving lane, east-side dining circulation claims, volume-only storage claims, simultaneous oven/washer/service-door comfort claims, unsupported ceiling drops, and construction readiness inferred from a green renderer.

**Keep unresolved:** site survey and actual mechanisms may invalidate the proposed small shoe unit. The 15mm chair-to-standing-user gap at the extreme +450 state is an adjacency result, not a comfort margin or bypass width. The drawings intentionally preserve that risk. Actual approved geometry and household operation must govern final joinery.

The under-hob oven positioning study in README.md is worth a vendor mockup: its translated proxy clears the sampled service sweep by 87.2mm, but still overlaps the washer user by 39,700mm². KEEP it as a product/layout test; REJECT a claim that two people can now use oven and washer together. No installed oven or service movement is shown in the renders.
