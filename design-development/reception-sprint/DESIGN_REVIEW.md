**Recommendation:** A as the coherent whole-home lead, with the shared measured layout refinements. Borrow B's modest linear pendant only if an actual lighting mockup gives a better dining focus without glare or a crowded ceiling. This optional hybrid is **not a third rendered alternative or a selected purchase**. P1/F1 remains the preferred connection to investigate; A/P0 is the fully developed fallback. Do not sacrifice a safe appliance/serving sequence to an attractive wider view.

**Reject:** historical K12/K4 coordinate imports, a new fixed divider in the serving lane, east-side dining circulation claims, volume-only storage claims, simultaneous oven/washer/service-door comfort claims, unsupported ceiling drops, and construction readiness inferred from a green renderer.

**Keep unresolved:** site survey and actual mechanisms may invalidate the proposed small shoe unit. The 15mm chair-to-standing-user gap at the extreme +450 state is an adjacency result, not a comfort margin or bypass width. The drawings intentionally preserve that risk. Actual approved geometry and household operation must govern final joinery.
