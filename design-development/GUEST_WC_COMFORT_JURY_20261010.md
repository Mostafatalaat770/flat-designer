## What would make a final guest WC genuinely enjoyable?

1. Trial two *actual* 350×180 and ~450×250 handbasins with their specific faucet spout projection, water flow and internal bowl depth. Larger outside dimension alone may not improve handwashing; there are shallow-but-deep-bowl specialized designs.
2. Confirm actual **trap/drain/valve** wall center, pipe chase, whether a basin of 450mm width is legally/safely hung on wall substrate with maintenance access and no sharp body contact.
3. Dimension reception door in both open/closed states, finished clear opening, 656.7mm nominal source + frame tolerance. Check WC seat and sink cleaning while another user operates door.
4. Mirror placement, hygienic hand drying / towel grab, toilet roll, deodorant and easy-clean access; do not add décor that clutters this 2.37m² model space.
5. Source a low-glare real task luminaire with qualified IP/electrician approval, suitable CRI and distribution, no false downlight simulation; verify actual fan extraction and door undercut.
6. Choose real porcelain sample and grout from Egypt, wet wipe resistance and matching warm taupe/walnut/nickel to full bathrooms; not screenshot-pick colours.
