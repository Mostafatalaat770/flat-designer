# K4 HVAC Design Brief

Status: **DESIGN BRIEF / LOAD CALCULATION + MEP DESIGN REQUIRED**

Verified clear ceiling target: **2.50 m**.  
K4 explicitly rejects lowering the whole reception ceiling.

## Conditioned zones

Approximate modeled room areas:
- Living + dining RECP-01: **47.46 m²**
- Open kitchen KIT-01: **8.45 m²**
- Combined open social field: approximately **55.9 m²** before considering circulation overlap / heat-load methodology
- Master bedroom: **14.68 m²**
- Bedroom 1: **12.22 m²**
- Bedroom 2: **12.29 m²**

Bathrooms, dressing and private hall should be handled as transfer/extract/support spaces rather than blindly sized as independent comfort zones.

## Preferred concept

### Social zone

Preferred:
- Concealed/slim-duct solution **only if** it can be concentrated in localized service zones while preserving the main 2.50 m reception ceiling.
- Supply/return locations should serve living/dining effectively without blowing directly at sofa/dining occupants.
- Kitchen comfort AC remains separate from the cooker-hood exhaust system.

Fallback:
- High-quality high-wall split / multiple split arrangement if concealed equipment would require a visually heavy blanket ceiling or unacceptable service access.

### Bedrooms

Preferred:
- Independent room control.
- Avoid routing one bedroom through another room's ceiling where possible.
- Place indoor units / slots to avoid direct air throw onto the sleeper's head/body.
- Condensate routes must have maintainable falls and access.

## Load-calculation inputs required

Do not select tonnage from area alone. The engineer must use:
- exterior design temperature / local climate basis
- façade orientation
- glazing dimensions and solar exposure
- glazing U-value / SHGC if available
- wall/roof construction
- floor level and roof exposure
- occupancy
- lighting and equipment gains
- open-kitchen cooking gains
- infiltration / ventilation assumptions
- diversity / zoning
- selected indoor design temperature and humidity targets

## Ceiling / indoor-unit rule

Earlier feasibility work showed why the nominal ~130 mm gypsum void cannot be treated as proof of ducted-AC feasibility.

K4 rule:
1. Keep the main reception ceiling at the verified **2.50 m clear** design plane.
2. Reserve deeper localized pockets only after the exact indoor unit, plenum, drain, insulation, duct radius and access panel are selected.
3. Place service access where maintenance can occur without dismantling fixed joinery.
4. Do not show speculative soffits as final architecture.

## Kitchen ventilation

Cooker hood is a separate extract system.
Preferred K4 route:
- 60/90 cm wall hood over west hob
- short ~1.19 m plan route toward service-terrace boundary
- manufacturer-sized duct, currently 150 mm round as a strong Bosch reference
- minimize bends and rough internal duct surfaces
- final external termination and make-up air to be engineered

Do not connect kitchen grease exhaust to comfort-AC ductwork.

## Bathrooms

- Provide mechanical exhaust where natural ventilation is insufficient.
- Exhaust controls can use timed / humidity-assisted logic if locally suitable.
- Prevent bathroom exhaust discharge from re-entering nearby glazing/intakes.

## Acoustic target

Equipment should be selected for comfortable normal-speed operation, not only headline maximum capacity.
The design should avoid:
- high air velocity at slots
- undersized ducts
- sharp bends immediately at plenums
- vibration transfer to gypsum/joinery
- locating noisy indoor units directly over beds

## Deliverables required from MEP designer

1. Room-by-room heat-load calculation.
2. Equipment schedule.
3. Indoor/outdoor unit locations.
4. Refrigerant / condensate routes.
5. Supply / return duct sizes and air quantities.
6. Slot/grille dimensions and throw direction.
7. Ceiling sections at every localized service zone.
8. Maintenance/access-panel locations.
9. Electrical requirements.
10. Coordination drawing with lighting, kitchen hood, curtains and joinery.

## Decision rule

If concealed HVAC can be achieved with **localized, maintainable service zones** while leaving most of the apartment at 2.50 m, keep concealed HVAC.

If it requires a large low ceiling or inaccessible maintenance, choose a cleaner split-system solution instead. Daily comfort and maintainability beat hiding equipment at any cost.
