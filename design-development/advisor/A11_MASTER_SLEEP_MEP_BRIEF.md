# A11 Master Bedroom — Sleep-first Lighting, Quiet HVAC and Electrical Coordination

Status: **DESIGN BRIEF / MEP REQUIRED**. Architectural source Type B v0.2.2, clear finished ceiling target **2500 mm**. This note does not authorize service runs, hidden duct units or furniture procurement.

## Owner / family daily comfort first

- Bed: retain **1870×2220 mm** nominal furniture envelope plus two **450×400 mm** nightstands in all candidate scenes. Mattress and actual frame/overhang dimensions are **TBD**.
- Dressing in separate WIC: two **1250×600 mm** opposing wardrobes. Bedroom drawer chest (1000×400) and dressing bench (1000×350) are **optional** until actual clothes/linen/storage inventory and living routine are assessed. A11 first tests *deleting only bench*, preserving chest.
- Prefer quiet, uncluttered foot path, direct bathroom night route, avoid sharp protruding corners beside walking routes, leave extra floor rather than automatically buy accent chairs/furniture.
- Preserve bedtime privacy, light control and calm wall palette over eye-catching novelty.

## Lighting circuit zones and controls (locations pending measured plan)

1. **General low-glare:** dimmable warm approximately **3000 K** indirect or carefully aimed track/fixture, not a dense grid. Single switch by room entry and usable from bed if electrician confirms two-way wiring.
2. **Independent reading:** left and right bedside adjustable lights, each independently switched, no high-angle light directly toward sleeper. Plan outlets/sockets near both nightstands.
3. **Night walking:** very dim, shielded floor/path light toward dressing/bathrooms on a separate circuit or integrated controllable dimming (no light pointed at bed); motion sensor is optional and must have override.
4. **Dressing mirror:** even task illumination, high colour fidelity, glare managed; cabinet LED strip optional and triggered by actual door system after A9 hardware approval.
5. **Curtain system:** practical full blackout for master bedroom windows plus sheer privacy option. Test track/pocket, pelmet and edge light leakage **at the actual window position**; don't assume living room's full-height glazing specification applies to the bedroom.

**No device count, lumen target, wattage, circuit design or position is final** until architect/electrician supply an exact reflected ceiling and wiring drawing, dimmer/driver compatibility and room mock-up under daylight and night.

## Quiet cooling / ventilation gate

- Calculate actual master heat gain by orientation, exterior wall/window exposure, shading, area, occupied sleeping hours, outside humidity and real occupancy. Do not size by floor m² alone.
- Prefer a **quiet wall split** by default if indoor unit can avoid a direct air jet toward the sleeping person's face/bed; select model only after acoustic, airflow and service requirements are known.
- A concealed unit is **not permitted by this brief**. 2630 mm underside-of-slab reference and target 2500 mm finished clear leave nominal **130mm**, smaller than many concealed unit bodies once ducts and condensate fall are included. Engineer must prove actual equipment sectional fit and future maintenance, not render a fictional hidden unit.
- Provide external unit, condensate trap/fall, power isolator, clean filter access, vibration and night-noise mitigation. Bedroom cooling must not share kitchen extractor air duct.
- Bathroom extraction (master ensuite) separately required and must not create a noisy route through sleeper’s headboard wall.

## Drawings to release before electrical first-fix

- Measured bed/wall/nightstand dimensions, real windows/doors and high/low power outlets with clear reach.
- RCP showing glare-safe fixtures, dimming groups, driver service access, curtain controls and switches.
- AC heat-load report, wall penetration/condensate/external location, measured sound and manufacturer drawing.
- Wardrobe hardware + integrated lighting coordination with WIC folding/sliding mechanism.
- Product shortlist, local pricing and repair access: owner chooses finish after physical mock-up.
- Final night route test with bedroom door, ensuite door and any drawer/bench open.

**Decision gate:** Treat visual calm, light and acoustic comfort as essential construction design. Bench and optional decor are deferrable purchases; finish-grade fixtures and lighting/control rough-in are not.
