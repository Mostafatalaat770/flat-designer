# J1 Integrated Candidate — current best whole-apartment direction

## Status

**DESIGN PREFERENCE: KEEP / DEVELOP**

**CONSTRUCTION STATUS: NOT APPROVED**

J1 combines the best reviewed studies without changing the canonical source geometry. Every architectural intervention remains render-only/exploratory until structural, MEP, waterproofing, developer and site constraints are verified.

## What J1 keeps

- **Kitchen E2:** full removal of the modeled kitchen/reception separator; 1800 × 900 mm longitudinal storage island; west cooking wall; three 600 mm tall modules; integrated-fridge direction.
- **Kitchen circulation:** approximately 1119 mm west work aisle and approximately 1150 mm south/tall-unit aisle after the final island shift.
- **Kitchen plumbing:** island-sink centre approximately 108 mm from the earlier sink centre, materially reducing the amount of wet-service relocation versus a remote island.
- **Guest WC F1:** existing narrow opening retained for now; door leaf studied opening outward; 350 × 180 mm compact handrinse envelope.
- **Family bath F1/I2:** modeled opening widened to 800 mm for sliding/pocket study; 1500 × 750 mm family bathtub.
- **Master ensuite F1/I2:** modeled opening widened to 800 mm for pocket-door study; 1500 × 800 mm walk-in shower.
- **HVAC G2:** preserve most of the 2.50 m clear ceiling; use compact local service pockets rather than a long room-crossing soffit.
- **Laundry H1:** reserve 600 × 650 mm washer on the service-terrace west wall, conditional on weather/manufacturer/MEP approval.
- **Entry/dining H2:** shift the full dining set 150 mm north, improving the modeled open-entry-door-to-chair pinch from about 811 mm to about 961 mm.
- **Bedrooms/private hall:** retain the already-developed layouts. No additional wall demolition found enough daily-value gain to justify risk.

## Hard gates before construction

1. Confirm kitchen/reception separator and bathroom opening edges are non-structural and free of concealed services.
2. Confirm developer/authority permission for all wall/opening changes.
3. Complete kitchen gas, hood/exhaust and make-up-air strategy.
4. Confirm island sink/dishwasher supply, drainage, access and floor/wall routing.
5. Complete HVAC room-by-room heat load, indoor/outdoor unit selection, condensate and service-access drawings.
6. Confirm exact bathroom clear openings after frames/pocket systems; modeled 800 mm wall gaps are not 813 mm clear openings.
7. Complete bath waterproofing, falls, drains, glass/screen, anti-slip and plumbing shop drawings.
8. Confirm service-terrace washer is permitted by selected manufacturer and protected from weather, with suitable drain and electrical protection.
9. Verify all dimensions on site before joinery/appliance procurement.

## Planning references used as design guidance

- NKBA legacy/public work-aisle planning guidance: 1067 mm one cook, 1219 mm multiple cooks.
- NKBA legacy/public bath doorway planning guidance: 813 mm recommended clear opening.
- NKBA 5th Edition released 2026; J1 treats NKBA as professional planning guidance, not Egyptian law.
- Daikin compact concealed-unit references demonstrate that 200 mm-high equipment can require about 240 mm ceiling void; G2 reserves roughly 290 mm vertically, but exact equipment and service clearances remain TBD.

## Decision

J1 is the **current best whole-apartment design direction** because it improves family connection, entry circulation, master daily use and kitchen utility without introducing bedroom demolition or a blanket lowered ceiling.

Do not merge J1 into canonical/master as approved construction geometry until the hard gates above are closed.
