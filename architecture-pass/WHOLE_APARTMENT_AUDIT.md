## Doorway interpretation

The model's narrow internal openings are not automatically illegal; exact construction, frames, local code and approval conditions must be verified. For professional planning we flag clear openings below the public NKBA 813 mm doorway recommendation for review rather than silently accepting them.

## Design principle

Prefer a surgical change that solves a real daily-use problem over demolition for visual drama. Any wall move must produce measurable improvement in at least two of: circulation, usable storage, appliance operation, natural light/visual connection, privacy, or family use.
