## Planning reference

The public NKBA bath planning guideline recommends at least 813 mm clear doorway opening. The modeled 800 mm wall gaps in F1 are therefore **not claimed as 813 mm clear** after frames/door systems; exact clear dimensions remain TBD. Local Egyptian/project requirements control.

## HVAC / ceiling finding

Verified apartment heights:
- slab-to-slab: 2.75 m
- reference/finished level to underside concrete: 2.63 m
- planned clear after gypsum: 2.50 m
- nominal service void: ~130 mm

That 130 mm void does **not** establish concealed-duct HVAC feasibility. Reference slim ducted units are already around 190–200 mm high, before required installation space, drainage, insulation, duct transitions and access. The next study should therefore test **localized deeper soffits / service zones**, not a blanket lower ceiling.
