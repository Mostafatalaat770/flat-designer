# Design / engineering review checklist

This checklist is mandatory for every developed apartment option. It distinguishes **code/authority**, **manufacturer**, **professional planning guidance**, and **project comfort targets**. A render can never satisfy the first two by itself.

## A. Architecture and approvals

- [ ] Wall classification verified from structural/as-built drawings.
- [ ] Any opening/removal signed off by a structural engineer where required.
- [ ] Compound/developer alteration rules checked.
- [ ] No façade, shaft, riser, structural element or common service altered without approval.
- [ ] Fire/egress implications reviewed where relevant.

## B. Circulation and doors

- Project comfort target: 900 mm clear circulation where practical.
- Project review threshold: 750 mm; values below this need an explicit reason and direct review.
- NKBA legacy/public doorway planning reference: 813 mm clear minimum; accessibility-oriented guidance is wider. Local code/approval controls.
- Door leaves, handles, drawers and appliance doors are checked in their operating positions, not only closed footprints.

## C. Kitchen

- NKBA planning target: 1067 mm one-cook work aisle; 1219 mm multiple-cook work aisle where applicable.
- Refrigerator landing/prep support checked against selected appliance and planning guidance.
- Sink, dishwasher, waste, prep and hob sequence tested.
- Dishwasher adjacent to sink unless a documented alternative is superior.
- Gas hob: selected manufacturer cutout, heat clearances, combustion-air/ventilation and extraction route required before approval.
- Worktop continuity, corner hardware, drawer conflicts and appliance service access checked.
- Refrigerator hinge, full door/drawer opening, ventilation, plug access and pull-out path checked.
- Washer niche/body/front/hoses/drain/supply/socket/service clearances checked against selected model.

## D. Bathrooms / WC

- Door swing versus basin/WC/shower/tub checked in use positions.
- Standing space and approach checked, not just fixture footprints.
- Waterproofing zones, floor falls, drain positions and service access coordinated.
- Mirror/light/towel/toilet-paper reach reviewed.
- Slip resistance and wet-area material suitability verified before procurement.

## E. Bedrooms / dressing

- Real outer bed-frame dimensions used, not mattress-only dimensions.
- Wardrobe door/drawer operating depth and dressing standing zone checked.
- Bedside circulation, sockets, lighting, curtain stack and glazing access checked.
- Furniture anchoring requirements checked for tall storage.

## F. Reception / dining

- Main family routes remain clear with chairs pulled for normal use.
- Dining chair pull-out and table pedestal/knee space checked with selected products.
- Sofa/coffee-table reach gap and TV sightlines checked at actual seat height.
- Terrace/glazing access and curtain stacks remain functional.
- Storage hierarchy is useful before decorative density is added.

## G. MEP / environment

- AC concept coordinated with verified 2.50 m finished ceiling and 130 mm service void; void depth alone does not prove duct feasibility.
- Lighting circuits/controls, socket locations and appliance loads coordinated.
- Plumbing/drain/gas routes and shutoff access coordinated.
- Kitchen extraction and bathroom ventilation routes verified.
- Acoustic, odor, glare and daylight consequences reviewed when spaces are opened.

## H. Procurement / construction gate

No item is construction-ready until:
- exact SKU or custom fabrication drawing exists;
- manufacturer installation manual has been checked;
- site dimensions/tolerances are measured;
- relevant engineer/contractor coordination is complete;
- status is promoted from EXPLORATORY/TBD to VERIFIED or APPROVED with evidence.

References used as planning inputs include NKBA Kitchen & Bath Planning Guidelines (5th edition current in 2026), current Egyptian codes/authority requirements via HBRC, and selected manufacturer installation instructions. These do not substitute for project-specific professional sign-off.
